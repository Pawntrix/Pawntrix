Smart by Design

Six Rules.
One Board.

01

Illegal Move Detection

The clock pauses the instant an illegal move is played and only resumes once the piece is correctly returned. No disputes. No guesswork.

02

Stalemate Recognition

Pawntrix detects stalemate in real time and halts the clock automatically. The game ends precisely when it should.

03

Castling Enforcement

The king must be picked up first — every time. Any attempt to castle by touching the rook is flagged immediately by the board.

04

Promotion Handling

The clock keeps running through promotion until the pawn is removed and the new piece is placed. No free time, no shortcuts.

05

3-Fold Repetition

Position tracking built right in. Pawntrix monitors repeated positions and enforces the draw rule automatically — no scoresheets needed.

06

Position Verification

The clock won't start if a single piece is out of place. Every game begins correctly, because Pawntrix checks before you do.
